Booking Platform – Final Working Policies & Pricing Rules
Updated Management Reference Document

1. Booking & Payment
Booking confirmation: A booking becomes Confirmed only after successful payment. Flow: Booking Created → Pending Payment → Payment Successful → Confirmed.

Unpaid/Pending bookings: An unpaid booking remains Pending for 30 minutes. If payment is not completed, it automatically expires and the room/time slot is released.

Payment reminder: A reminder is sent/displayed at approximately 2–5 minutes after booking creation.

Confirmation email: Sent after successful payment and should include booking reference, room, date/time, duration, amount paid, payment status, and applicable policies.

Booking reminders: Send reminders 1 day and 1 hour before the scheduled booking.

2. Operating Hours & Availability
Operating schedule: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM.

Weekends: Closed. If weekend availability is introduced later, use the weekday hourly rate initially unless demand data justifies a premium.

Public holidays: Closed and unavailable for booking.

Automatic blocking: The system should automatically block weekends, public holidays, and administrator-designated closed dates.

Manual closures: Administrators may close rooms/dates for maintenance, private events, company activities, emergencies, or other business needs.

3. Weekly & Monthly Plans
Weekly plan: 10 hours per week.

Monthly plan: 40 hours per month.

Weekly discount: 10% below the standard hourly rate.

Monthly discount: 20% below the standard hourly rate.

Consecutive bookings: Multiple consecutive days or weeks may be booked in one transaction, subject to availability.

Maximum duration: No fixed overall cap. Customers may book as many hours/days as needed subject to availability and operating hours. Maximum available window per day is 10 hours.

4. Membership
Status: On Hold. All membership sections and features are not currently being implemented. This section is retained for future reference only and should not be active in the system.

5. Cancellation & Refunds
Customer cancellations: Completed bookings are 100% non-refundable.

Non-refundable notice: The policy should be clearly displayed before payment and acknowledged by the customer.

Late cancellation fee: The separate 50% late-cancellation fee is removed because the booking is already non-refundable.

Emergency exceptions: Genuine emergencies may be reviewed case-by-case by management.

6. Rescheduling
Notice: Customers should request a reschedule at least 24 hours before the original booking.

First reschedule: One qualifying reschedule is free.

Second/late reschedule: A 10% fee based on the original booking value may apply.

Rescheduling window: One-off hourly/daily bookings may be moved within 30 days of the original date. Weekly/monthly plan sessions should remain within the applicable plan period or immediately following approved period.

Higher replacement price: Customer pays the difference.

Lower replacement price: No cash refund; the difference becomes account credit for a future eligible booking.

Weekly/monthly plans: Individual sessions may be rescheduled under the same basic rules, subject to availability.

7. No-Show Policy
No-show charge: 100% of the booking value is forfeited.

Exceptions: Genuine emergencies may be reviewed case-by-case by management. No automatic emergency exception is granted by the system.

8. Business-Initiated Cancellation / Room Unavailability
Business cancellation: If the business cancels a confirmed paid booking, the customer may choose between a full refund or free rescheduling.

Room becomes unavailable: If a confirmed paid room becomes unavailable because of maintenance, emergency closure, operational issues, or another business reason, the customer receives the same choice: full refund or free rescheduling.

No rescheduling fee: Business-initiated changes never incur a customer rescheduling fee.

9. Recommended Booking State Flow
Created → Pending: Customer selects a room/time and proceeds to payment.

Pending → Confirmed: Payment succeeds within 30 minutes.

Pending → Expired: Payment is not completed within 30 minutes; availability is released.

Confirmed → Completed: Booking time passes successfully.

Confirmed → Rescheduled: Customer uses an eligible reschedule.

Confirmed → Cancelled: Customer cancels; no cash refund under the standard policy.

Confirmed → Business Cancelled: Customer chooses full refund or free rescheduling.

Confirmed → No-show: Customer fails to attend; 100% of booking value is forfeited.
